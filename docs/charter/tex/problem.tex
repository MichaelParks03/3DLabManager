The Senior Design labs contain a large inventory of equipment and other items that are frequently used by students. However, many students are unfamiliar with the lab and the locations of many items. As a result, the lab manager receives many inquiries about where different items are stored. These inquires both slow down the progress of students as they await a response, and distract the lab manager from other responsibilities. Moreover, many creative projects created in these labs remain unknown to students and others outside of the lab.
