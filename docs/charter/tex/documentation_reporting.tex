\subsection{Major Documentation Deliverables}
All major documentation deliverables will be versioned at minimum with a revision history table in each document. Revision numbering will begin at 0.1 and increment by 0.1. A major revision may be designated and will begin by finding the ceiling of the current version number. Incremental changes will be tracked through git or another tool integrated into an editor.
\subsubsection{Project Charter}
There will be two milestone releases for the project charter: a first draft at the end of sprint one (Sep 28, 2026) and a final draft at the end of 8. This document should not need frequent revision, however will be updated as deemed necessary by group consensus.

\subsubsection{System Requirements Specification}
The initial version of the System Requirements Specification will be completed by the end of sprint two (Oct 19, 2026). This document will codify requirements gathered from relevant stakeholders during sprint one as recorded in our requirements spreadsheet. Most of this document should remain static, however customer requirements may move throughout the project as a result of user testing. Substantial changes in scope or features will be updated during the sprint review/planning stage following stakeholder consultation. The final revision of this document will be sumitted with the completed project in April of 2027.

\subsubsection{Architectural Design Specification}
The Architectural Design Specification will be completed at the end of sprint three (Nov 9, 2026). Barring major changes to requirements or hardware availability, we do not anticipate substantial modifications to the architectural documentation. In the event of a architectural redesign, relevant decisions will be reflected in this document. The final iteration will be submitted in April of 2027.

\subsubsection{Detailed Design Specification}
The Detailed Design Specification will be completed with the wrap up of Sprint 5 in early February. As implementation progresses in Senior Design 2, implementation details may change substantially. Following the completion of successive sprints the team will compare implementation details to the documentation and ammend any discrepancies. The final copy of this document will be submitted in April of 2027 with the completed project.

\subsection{Recurring Sprint Items}

\subsubsection{Product Backlog}
Features will be added to the product backlog in consultation with the project owner and the System Requirements Specification. Changes proposed by stakeholders will ultimately by voted upon by the development team before adoption. The product backlog will be tracked by the project Jira board.

\subsubsection{Sprint Planning}
There will be a total of eight sprits across both semesters, with one elapsed from the first revision of this document. Planning for the next sprint will occur in discussion with the project owner during the end of the previous sprint.

\subsubsection{Sprint Goal}
During the sprint review stakeholder consultation the development team shall ellicit a sprint goal from the product owner. Failing a clear directive from the owner, the team shall identify necessary technical debt to remove to improve reliability.

\subsubsection{Sprint Backlog}
After the determination of the sprint goal, the group shall discuss which features most directly accomplish the goal and are achievable within the available timeframe. The sprint backlog will be tracked in a Jira scrum board alongside the product backlog.

\subsubsection{Task Breakdown}
Group members will first volunteer for tasks, remaining tasks will be reasonably divided amongst team members which did not volunteer such that the overall assignment of work is roughly equal.
